Postiz for X

Tweets you scroll past on x.com that get at least 3x their author's usual likes are saved to your Postiz library (Growth, X Inspiration). The extension only reads the pages you open; it never scrolls, clicks or posts on X.

Find the key in Postiz: Settings, Public API.

X scheduler (X Premium)

Posts you queue in Postiz (Growth, X Inspiration, "Scheduled in X") are put into X's own scheduler when you click Sync. X then publishes them at their time, even when this computer is off. Text only: no media and no threads. A post meant for another X account waits until x.com is signed in as that account. The extension opens x.com, fills the composer and its schedule dialog, and clicks "Schedule", never "Post". Use X in English while syncing.

Checking the queue…

The toolbar badge shows the posts waiting for the X scheduler (orange), otherwise the tweets saved to your library today (purple).
